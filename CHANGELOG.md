# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Built-in world reset: a fresh overworld, Nether and End per attempt, with linked portals and deletion of old worlds.
- Per-player death counter with skin faces in the action bar, tab list and sidebar.
- Boss bar timer with auto-start or countdown start.
- Splits with comparison against the best time; records for best time, wins, best splits and hall of shame.
- Death effects: lightning, title with the player's face, random roast line, attempt summary.
- Victory fireworks and title.
- `/catchup` to gear up late joiners (admin or server-average tier).
- End conditions: first death or all dead.
- Fixed or random seed, `/run seed`, `/run reset [seed]`, `/run cancel`.
- English and Spanish messages; translatable through `lang/*.yml`.
- Granular permissions and `/br reload`.
